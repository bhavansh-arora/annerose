Hi Anne,

Thanks again for saying yes to a few ideas. Your current site already has the right things on it: your credentials, a strong set of testimonials, and a clear line, "Embrace the knowing within." So rather than describe ideas, we built them out using your own content, words and photos, so you can see how they'd look and work.

**Here's the preview:** [LINK]

It runs on phones, tablets and desktop. Here's what we added and how each piece helps:

**1. A homepage that leads with your results.** "95% or more of goals in 3 months or less," the 102% team result from Magellan, your 13 designations and "lovingly kick butt" all appear near the top now, instead of further down the page. A visitor who arrives curious can see quickly that you're the real thing.

**2. The Clarity Compass, a 60-second fit quiz.** Four quick questions guide a visitor to Leadership, Life Coaching, Career or Workshops, and ask whether they'd prefer online or in person in Toronto and how soon they want to start. One click then sends the answers to you. People arrive knowing what they want, so your first conversations start further along.

**3. An enquiry form that sorts enquiries for you.** It's four short steps (what they need, online or in person, timing, contact details). Each enquiry reaches you with the details already filled in, and you can see at a glance who is ready now and who is just exploring. The form can connect to the tools you already use for an automatic confirmation email, a booking link and reminders, so you don't have to chase anyone by hand.

**4. Clearer pages for online and in-person sessions.** One-on-one coaching now has its own page that explains both formats, your four-step process (Connect, Roadmap, Deep Work, Results) and the questions people usually ask before booking.

**5. All your testimonials on one page.** We gathered all 15 client stories onto one page that visitors can filter by leadership, career or life, so a VP and a career-changer can each find someone like them.

**6. A waitlist for the Spring Reset.** Your six-week program gets its own spot on the site, and people can join the waitlist for the next cohort all year round, so interest builds before you open registration.

**7. Ignite!, your speaking and your articles.** Your book, your #1 speaker rating at Toyota Canada and your blog articles now support each other, so each one helps make the case for working with you.

None of this replaces what you have. It can sit on your current domain, and the blog links keep pointing to your existing posts.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call this week? I can walk you through it and hear what you'd change.
